## 2024-05-24 - AnimatedTestimonials Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only navigation buttons in custom components (like Framer Motion carousels) often lack accessible names, making them invisible to screen readers.
**Action:** Always verify that `<button>` elements containing only icons (e.g., `<IconArrowLeft />`) have descriptive `aria-label` attributes to ensure full keyboard and screen reader accessibility.
